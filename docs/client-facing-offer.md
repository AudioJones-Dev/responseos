# Client-Facing Offer

## Headline

> **ResponseOS turns missed demand into booked revenue.**

We capture every call, qualify every lead, automate the follow-up, book the appointment, send the quote, and prove the recovered revenue every month — so you stop paying for marketing that walks straight into voicemail.

## What it is

ResponseOS is the AI Revenue Recovery Platform for service businesses. Built on the OFFER + RECOVER frameworks. Operated by AJ Digital. White-labelable for partners.

## What you get

- **Missed-call recovery in under 60 seconds.** Every missed call gets an AI text back before the homeowner calls the next contractor on their list.
- **AI inbound answering.** A trained agent answers, qualifies, and books — 24/7 — using your business hours, service area, and pricing rules.
- **Self-schedule + self-quote.** Customers book directly from a link or web widget; quotes ship by SMS or email; deposits captured on the spot.
- **Verified ROI report.** Monthly summary showing recovered revenue, booked appointments, jobs won, and ROI multiple — with the evidence to back it up.

## Sample contract clause — Recovered Demand Fee

> **Recovered Demand Fee.** In addition to the Monthly Platform Fee, Client agrees to pay AJ Digital a Recovery Fee for each Verified Outcome generated through the Revenue Recovery System during the billing period. A "Verified Outcome" means either:
> (a) a booked appointment attributable to a tracked call, message, or workflow event initiated by the system and not canceled within 24 hours; or
> (b) recovered revenue collected from a customer interaction that would otherwise have been missed, abandoned, or unworked based on the historical baseline mutually agreed in Exhibit A.
>
> AJ Digital will provide event-level evidence, timestamps, source attribution, and booking/payment references with each invoice. Disputed outcomes must be submitted in writing within 30 days.

## Vertical roadmap

**First vertical (live with v0.1/v0.2):** Home services — HVAC, roofing, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, contractors. Highest demand for missed-call recovery + self-quote + self-schedule, lowest compliance burden, fastest to ROI.

**Later verticals (only after compliance hardening):**
- **Med spas** — requires Privacy-hardened mode minimum; vetted disclosure scripts; jurisdictional consent rules.
- **Real estate** — agent + brokerage workflows; lead routing across teams; MLS connector evaluation.
- **Auto repair** — service-write-up flows; parts/labor quote engine; vehicle history capture.
- **Legal / medical** — HIPAA-ready mode required; full vendor BAA chain in force; on a per-tenant lock-down. **Not in v0.1/v0.2 scope.**

Each later vertical is gated on (a) the relevant compliance lane being production-verified for at least one pilot, (b) a vertical-specific qualification rubric and quote/booking template set, and (c) tested CRM connectors for that vertical's tooling.

## The 9 KPIs we report on

ResponseOS reports nine KPIs every month per workspace. These are the numbers that go in the client portal hero, the PDF export, and the outcome-fee invoice.

1. **Recovered Revenue (estimated)** — qualified recovered leads × avg job value × estimated close rate.
2. **Recovered Revenue (verified)** — closed-won jobs traceable to ResponseOS-handled events.
3. **ROI Multiple** — recovered revenue / monthly system cost.
4. **Missed Calls Recovered** — captured + followed up vs total missed.
5. **Qualified Leads Captured** — lead events scored qualified within the period.
6. **Appointments Booked** — confirmed bookings tied to recovered leads.
7. **Quote Requests Created (and Sent)** — top-of-funnel quoting volume + delivery rate.
8. **Average Response Time** — first reply to inbound demand signal (target: under 60s).
9. **Admin Hours Saved** — vs the manual-follow-up baseline established at onboarding.

## Why ResponseOS, not "an AI receptionist"

A receptionist answers calls. ResponseOS recovers revenue. The difference is the orchestration layer — qualification scoring, quote-to-schedule state machine, ROI attribution, weekly reporting, and the outcome-fee engine — none of which exist in a calling-only product.
