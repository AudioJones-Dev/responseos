Run bridge and watchdog under independent supervisors with restart limits and operator-visible failure notifications. A restart does not release claims or retry uncertain commands. Verify database outage, bridge kill, watchdog kill, TLS failure and log collector loss. Do not deploy both on the same lifecycle or rely on the bridge to announce its own death. The watchdog's one-second scheduling delay is not a one-second termination SLA: database, paging and network delays apply.
