## No-effects qualification protocol (requires separate live authorization)

1. Record authorized test window, fictional scenario, witness, intended tenant/number, exact SHA/configuration hash and provider settings. Start the dedicated qualification assignment with the exact disabled SUPERVISED_PILOT profile and approved operating snapshot. Verify CRM/email remain disabled and the assignment is `qualification`.
2. Answer through Call Control without recording, AI, transcription, gather-AI, streaming or media fork. Complete the approved disclosure and receive its signed completion before one-digit DTMF gather. Silence, ambiguous input and refusal must never start the assistant.
3. In the authenticated console select the initialized capture belonging to this exact call and assignment. DTMF is provider input evidence, not the operator's consent record. Witness and append grant/refusal with disclosure, evidence and jurisdiction references; never backdate or fabricate an event.
4. For grant, verify durable consent and start intent precede the idempotent assistant-start command. Verify authenticated command response/conversation ID and signed cumulative-history generation/bounds. No retained content may precede the grant or exceed a verified interval.
5. For withdrawal, verify durable consent event/admission fence before stop command, and reject late protected content even if signed. Preserve previously retained evidence under its policy. Verify duplicate and out-of-order deliveries cannot restart capture or bypass the fence.
6. For normal completion, require signed conversation-end plus terminal events and the five-second history quiet window. Hangup alone is not a content boundary. Check revisions, hashes and superseding review behavior for late in-interval history.
7. Measure initialization p95 below 1,000 ms and every request below 1,500 ms. Record sample size and durations without caller content. Confirm frozen transcript reaches authenticated review within two minutes of hangup; record call/capture/assignment/review IDs and timing.
8. Attempt business-effect dispatch only through offline tests during this phase. Live qualification calls must stay permanently ineligible for CRM/email, including after the assignment ends and a later pilot is activated. End qualification explicitly, retaining its historical interval.
9. Record pass/fail separately for grant, refusal, silence, withdrawal, duplicates, delayed history, incomplete runtime and initialization timing. A failure blocks pilot authorization; it is not reclassified as a passing run.
