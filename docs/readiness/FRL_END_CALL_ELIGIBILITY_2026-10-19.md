## Exact eligibility rule

Operator authorization precedes inspection. Locate the stored capture/account and require the displayed exact provider-call ID and generation. Within the existing account/capture lock, fresh signature-bound original hangup evidence must match account, provider, event ID/type, call-control/session/leg, capture ID and generation before disconnection is confirmed. A fresh match disables dispatch and returns 200. Expired, erased or unverifiable bytes are never parsed as fresh proof, reconstructed, or displayed as a verified historical event.

An existing same-generation hangup claim is returned idempotently without resending, regardless of command outcome. A claim from any other generation is a veto against a new hangup for the capture. No retry control is offered after a claim. Before a NEW claim, all of these must hold: a qualification assignment; complete exact identifiers; no recorded call_disconnected_at; no EVIDENCE_FROZEN/TRANSFER_PENDING/TRANSFERRED lifecycle fence; no matching or unattributable hangup candidate; no prior hangup claim; and at least one retained authenticated exact-call/generation call.initiated, call.answered, call.speak.ended or call.gather.ended event. This is retained active-call evidence, not a claim of current carrier connectivity. Missing/expired/unverifiable active identity fails closed. Normalized Call.status is never consulted for eligibility or confirmation.

The one-shot uncertain intent is committed before dispatch. Recheck evidence, lifecycle, assignment, exact target and active identity under the second capture lock, excluding only that exact newly created claim from the prior-claim veto. Any newly unavailable evidence leaves the durable claim uncertain and sends nothing. No automatic resend occurs. Failed, uncertain or blocked calls require independent operator fallback; command acceptance is never disconnection.

Verified hangup processing records the capture_ended_at boundary even when a withdrawn STOPPED/REFUSED/AMBIGUOUS/TRANSFERRED/EVIDENCE_FROZEN state is preserved, and records call_disconnected_at only as described in the amendment below. The retained original is reverified under the capture lock before this update. capture_ended_at means content capture ended; it is not a dispatch veto. Withdrawal receipt, consent closure and downstream guards remain intact.

## Retention and ambiguity

Presentation states are fresh, expired, erased, absent and unverifiable. Only fresh has confirmed=true and event/time evidence. All others return null proof details. Expired/erased categories describe retained metadata candidates, not a verified link to this generation. Erasure deliberately clears provider-call correlation; the console may only report account-level erased/unattributable hangup evidence. Such a candidate blocks new End calls for that account, conservatively including unrelated captures, until an independently authorized resolution exists. No mapping or payload is retained to bypass erasure. A fresh exact hangup can still confirm its own capture. Unattributable or invalid hangup metadata also vetoes dispatch.

The account-level veto is an intentional availability cost. There is no UI override, force retry, expiry-based release or provider lookup. A recorded call_disconnected_at and one-shot claims survive loss of original proof; neither is displayed as freshly verified raw proof. If the original row and its association have already been erased/deleted without a lifecycle boundary, exact historical attribution cannot be recovered. Fresh active identity is still mandatory and ambiguity is not resolved by normalized status. This patch does not manufacture historical facts.

## Disconnection fact amendment (F1, migration 0022)

Owner approved F1 and local migration 0022 on October 19. Migration 0022 adds nullable CallCaptureSession.call_disconnected_at with no backfill; migrations 0001-0021 are unchanged. The column is written once, inside the capture lock, only while processing a fresh authenticated call.hangup whose retained original reverifies and matches the exact account, provider, call control id, session, leg, capture ID and generation, and whose payload is neither purged, pending erasure nor expired. A hangup from another leg in the same session, another session or leg, another capture or generation, or with missing client_state never records it. A trigger (guard_call_disconnection) rejects clearing or changing a recorded value; a later exact hangup does not overwrite it. The value is the carrier event's occurred_at.

Historical disconnection and currently verifiable raw proof are separate. The console shows "Disconnection: recorded ..." from call_disconnected_at, which does not expire, and separately "Current raw hangup proof" from the fresh/expired/erased/absent/unverifiable states, which keep their retention behaviour and never return details for non-fresh evidence. A recorded disconnection disables End call (reason call_disconnected) and suppresses the carrier fallback text, including after raw proof expires or is erased.

capture_ended_at, call.conversation.ended and FINALIZING no longer veto End call: after withdrawal, the AI conversation can end while the caller remains connected. EVIDENCE_FROZEN (reachable only after a hangup row exists), TRANSFER_PENDING and TRANSFERRED remain lifecycle vetoes. End call on a FINALIZING capture keeps FINALIZING so the ended conversation can still reconcile its final transcript; withdrawn captures keep their state as before. Terminal withdrawal, operator authorization, qualification-only scope, fresh exact active evidence and the one-shot uncertain claim are unchanged. Legacy captures that disconnected before 0022 have no recorded fact; any retained hangup row for them still vetoes dispatch through the raw-proof states.

Not changed: the account-wide unattributable-hangup veto (F2) is unresolved and awaits an owner scope decision; no lifetime or correlation-retention change is made. Whether the carrier echoes client_state on call.hangup (including caller-initiated hangups) is unverified; without it no fact is recorded and the retained hangup row keeps End call vetoed. Regressions: withdrawal then conversation.ended then End call dispatches exactly once; mismatched hangups record nothing; raw-proof expiry after a recorded disconnection keeps End call disabled; the recorded value cannot be cleared or changed. A legacy upgrade probe applies 0022 over historical rows in two session time zones on PostgreSQL 16/17/18.
